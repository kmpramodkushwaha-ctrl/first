Our Story

A Little Oven with a Big Heart

Antra Bakery began in a small home kitchen in Prathvipur Dhai — one oven, a family recipe book, and a belief that good food, made with patience, can brighten anyone's day.

Today, our ovens warm up before sunrise. Cakes are creamed by hand, breads are pulled out golden, and chai is always on the stove. Every order — big or small — leaves our counter only when it's something we'd proudly serve our own family.

The Hands Behind the Oven
Meet the Owner

Atul Kumar

Founder & Head Baker, Antra Bakery

I still remember my first tray of burnt biscuits and the neighbours who ate them anyway, smiling. That kindness is why Antra exists. What began as weekend experiments in a Prathvipur Dhai kitchen slowly became morning bakes for the whole town.

For me, every cake that leaves this counter carries a piece of my family's recipes — and a promise that it was made slowly, honestly, and with far too much love.

"Baking is my way of sharing happiness."

— Atul
