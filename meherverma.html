( 01 ) — About

The hand behind the face

"I don't paint faces — I reveal the one already there."

Nine years ago I started with a borrowed kit and one stubborn belief — that makeup should feel like skin and photograph like light. Today, from quiet bridal mornings in Chennai to flash-heavy editorial sets in Mumbai, that belief hasn't moved an inch.

My signature is restraint: bases that breathe, contour you can't find, a smoked eye built in twenty thin layers instead of two thick ones. The structure stays invisible. The glow does the talking.
